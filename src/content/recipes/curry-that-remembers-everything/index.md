### A Simmer With A Long Memory

The spices, once toasted, seem to recall every curry that came before them. The recipe insists on "letting the flavors develop overnight," though it never says what they develop into.

By morning, the curry appears entirely at peace with itself.

---

### Ingredients, Historically Informed

- 2 tbsp curry powder, well-traveled
- 1 can coconut milk, unusually calm
- 2 cups vegetables, cooperative
- 1 onion, carrying old grudges

---

### Method, Passed Down Somehow

1. Toast the spices until the kitchen remembers something.
2. Add onion and cook until the grudges soften.
3. Stir in coconut milk and vegetables, then simmer.
4. Let rest overnight, or until the curry feels resolved.

---

### Final Thoughts

The curry was deep, layered, and quietly wise about its own flavor. Best enjoyed the next day, once it's had time to think things over.

Overall rating: **9 out of 10 thoughtfully aged spices**.
