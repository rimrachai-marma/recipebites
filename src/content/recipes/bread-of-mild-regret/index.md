### A Dough With A Complicated Past

The recipe instructs the baker to "let the dough rest and reflect." It is unclear what the dough is meant to reflect on, though by hour two it appears to have reached some conclusions.

The yeast, notably, refuses to comment on the record.

---

### Ingredients, Reluctantly Provided

- 3 cups flour, recently betrayed by a sifter
- 1 packet yeast, keeping its opinions private
- 1 tsp salt, the only ingredient behaving normally
- Warm water, temperature negotiable

---

### Method, With Some Hesitation

1. Combine flour and yeast, allowing space for feelings.
2. Knead until the dough stops second-guessing itself.
3. Let rise until it has made peace with its shape.
4. Bake until golden, or until the regret becomes mild.

---

### Final Thoughts

The finished loaf was crusty, warm, and only slightly haunted. A triumphant bake, provided you don't ask the yeast too many questions.

Overall rating: **9 out of 10 quietly satisfied loaves**.
