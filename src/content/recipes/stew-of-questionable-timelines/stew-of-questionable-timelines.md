### A Simmer That Forgot What Day It Is

The recipe calls for "low heat, for either two hours or several days, depending on the stew's mood." The potatoes seem unbothered by this ambiguity.

Somewhere around hour one, the broth develops a distinct sense of nostalgia.

---

### Ingredients, Time-Optional

- 3 potatoes, patient by nature
- 2 carrots, mildly impatient
- 4 cups broth, occasionally reminiscent
- 1 bay leaf, keeping track of the hours

---

### Method, Loosely Timed

1. Brown the vegetables while ignoring the clock.
2. Add broth and bring to a contemplative simmer.
3. Let cook until the potatoes soften and the stew feels resolved.
4. Remove the bay leaf before it starts telling stories.

---

### Final Thoughts

The stew was rich, warming, and entirely unclear about how long it actually took to make. A comforting bowl for uncertain evenings.

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 mildly nostalgic potatoes**.
