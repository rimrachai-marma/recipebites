### Broth That Isn't Sure What It Wants To Be

This soup begins as a clear broth, then quietly becomes something thicker halfway through simmering. No explanation is offered, and none is expected.

The celery, for its part, remains steady throughout.

---

### Ingredients, Somewhat Undecided

- 1 onion, indecisive
- 2 stalks celery, dependable
- 3 cups broth, gradually thickening
- 1 tbsp flour, the quiet architect of the change

---

### Method, Evolving As It Goes

1. Sauté the onion until it makes up its mind.
2. Add celery and broth, then bring to a simmer.
3. Stir in flour and watch the texture shift.
4. Season and serve, regardless of what it decided to become.

---

### Final Thoughts

The soup was warm, hearty, and refreshingly unbothered by its own indecision. A good match for a day when nothing else feels certain either.

Overall rating: **7 out of 10 gently thickening broths**.
