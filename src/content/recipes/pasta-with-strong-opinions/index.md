### Noodles That Know Best

The recipe suggests "cooking the pasta al dente, or however the pasta insists." The garlic, upon hitting the pan, immediately takes charge of the entire dish.

By the time the sauce reduces, the noodles have developed firm culinary opinions.

---

### Ingredients, Under Advisement

- 1 lb pasta, self-assured
- 4 cloves garlic, clearly in charge
- 2 cups tomato sauce, easily persuaded
- Parmesan, for a final confident flourish

---

### Method, As Directed By The Pasta

1. Boil the pasta exactly as long as it demands.
2. Sauté garlic until it takes over the kitchen.
3. Add sauce and simmer until everyone agrees.
4. Toss with pasta and top generously with parmesan.

---

### Final Thoughts

The final dish was bold, garlicky, and utterly convinced of its own excellence. Hard to argue with, honestly.

Overall rating: **9 out of 10 self-assured garlic cloves**.
