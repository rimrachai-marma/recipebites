### Grains That Don't Fully Trust The Process

The rice, according to the recipe, should be "rinsed until the water runs clear, and until the rice believes you." This condition is difficult to verify.

The vegetables, meanwhile, keep a watchful distance from the sauce.

---

### Ingredients, Cautiously Combined

- 2 cups rice, skeptical
- 1 cup mixed vegetables, wary
- 3 tbsp soy sauce, persuasive
- 1 egg, fried and unbothered

---

### Method, With Reasonable Caution

1. Rinse the rice until it seems convinced.
2. Cook rice according to package instructions, or general trust.
3. Stir-fry vegetables until they relax slightly.
4. Combine everything and top with the fried egg.

---

### Final Thoughts

The bowl was satisfying, well-balanced, and only mildly distrustful of the sauce. A dependable lunch for skeptical days.

Overall rating: **7 out of 10 cautiously optimistic grains**.
