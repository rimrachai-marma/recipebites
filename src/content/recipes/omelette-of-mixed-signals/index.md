### Eggs That Won't Commit to a Direction

Every attempt at a clean fold gets interpreted, generously, as a suggestion. Somewhere between whisking and plating, this omelette decides it would actually prefer to be a scramble, and there's no talking it out of it.

> [!TIP]
> Lower heat than you think you need. A confident flame just speeds up the miscommunication.

---

### Filling Options, In Case It Changes Its Mind

| Filling   | Amount | Signal Sent                 |
| --------- | ------ | --------------------------- |
| Cheddar   | ½ cup  | Reassuring                  |
| Spinach   | 1 cup  | Mixed, but well-intentioned |
| Mushrooms | ½ cup  | Noncommittal                |
| Chives    | 1 tbsp | Trying to lighten the mood  |

---

### Method Checklist

- [ ] Whisk 3 eggs with a splash of milk until just combined
- [ ] Melt butter in a nonstick pan over low-medium heat
- [ ] Pour in eggs and let sit undisturbed for 20 seconds
- [ ] Gently push edges toward the center, tilting the pan to let egg flow underneath
- [ ] Add fillings to one half once the top is just barely set
- [ ] Attempt a fold; accept whatever shape results

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If it falls apart into scrambled eggs with extra steps, that's not a failure — that's just where the omelette landed today.

---

### Final Thoughts

Technically a scramble, emotionally an omelette. Tasted fine either way, which is really the only signal that mattered.

Overall rating: **6 out of 10 unresolved folds**.
