### A Dish That Refuses To Commit

The recipe begins by asking for "a casserole dish, or something casserole-adjacent." This is not clarified further. The cheese, upon melting, appears to develop a sense of superiority.

By the second layer, the dish has stopped pretending to have a plan.

---

### Ingredients, More Or Less

- 2 cups pasta, mildly suspicious of the sauce
- 1 cup cheese, increasingly confident
- 1 can tomatoes, undefeated
- Breadcrumbs, for a crunchy sense of closure

---

### Method, Approximately

1. Boil the pasta until it stops resisting.
2. Layer everything in a dish with conviction.
3. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs, then bake until golden.
4. Let rest for ten minutes, or until the steam calms down.

---

### Final Thoughts

The casserole emerged bubbling, unbothered, and somehow exactly what the fridge needed. A reliable dish for nights when decisions feel difficult.

Overall rating: **7 out of 10 quietly triumphant baking dishes**.
