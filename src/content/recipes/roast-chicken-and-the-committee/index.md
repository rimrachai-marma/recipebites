### A Roast Overseen By Several Vegetables

The recipe places the chicken in the oven "under the supervision of the surrounding root vegetables." The potatoes take this responsibility seriously.

By the final basting, the carrots have formed a subcommittee.

---

### Ingredients, Duly Appointed

- 1 whole chicken, the main subject
- 4 potatoes, supervisory
- 3 carrots, forming committees
- 2 tbsp butter, keeping everyone civil

---

### Method, By Unanimous Vote

1. Season the chicken and appoint the vegetables around it.
2. Roast until the committee reaches golden consensus.
3. Baste occasionally to maintain order.
4. Rest before carving, allowing final remarks.

---

### Final Thoughts

The roast was tender, well-organized, and thoroughly reviewed by its vegetable oversight committee. A dependable Sunday dinner, assuming the carrots don't call another meeting.

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 procedurally sound potatoes**.
