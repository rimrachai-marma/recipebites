### A Broth With Strong Personal Boundaries

The recipe opens by insisting the onions be "sweated, not sad." This distinction is never elaborated on. The stockpot, for its part, seems to have prior grievances with the stove.

By minute ten, the carrots have formed a small committee.

---

### Ingredients, Under Some Duress

- 1 onion, currently sad regardless of instructions
- 3 carrots, self-organizing
- 4 cups broth, origin unclear
- A bay leaf with a flair for the dramatic

---

### Method, As Negotiated

1. Sauté the onion until it reconsiders its life choices.
2. Add carrots and allow the committee meeting to conclude naturally.
3. Pour in broth and simmer until the bay leaf calms down.
4. Season to taste, or to whatever the soup demands.

---

### Final Thoughts

The final soup was rich, faintly opinionated, and refused to be reheated without complaint. A deeply satisfying bowl, assuming you can get the carrots to agree on anything.

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 mildly unionized vegetables**.
