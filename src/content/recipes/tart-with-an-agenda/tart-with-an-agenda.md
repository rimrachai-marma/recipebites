### Pastry With A Plan Of Its Own

The crust, once rolled out, appears to have very specific ideas about its final shape. The recipe instructs bakers to "work with the dough, not against it."

Nobody explains what the dough actually wants.

---

### Ingredients, Cooperative At Best

- 1 pie crust, opinionated
- 3 eggs, going along with it
- 1 cup cream, remarkably agreeable
- A handful of herbs, mostly bystanders

---

### Method, Negotiated Carefully

1. Roll out the crust and let it settle into shape.
2. Whisk the eggs and cream until unified.
3. Pour into the crust and add herbs diplomatically.
4. Bake until set, and until the tart seems satisfied.

---

### Final Thoughts

The tart came out golden, silky, and quietly pleased with itself. A dish best served to people who don't ask too many questions about the crust's motives.

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 politely demanding pastries**.
