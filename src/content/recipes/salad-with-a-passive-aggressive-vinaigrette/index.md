### A Dressing That Remembers Every Slight

It looks like a normal vinaigrette. It is not a normal vinaigrette. One bite in and it's clear this dressing has been keeping a running list, and today is the day it reads it aloud.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Taste the vinaigrette before adding it to the greens. Once it's mixed in, there's no smoothing things over.

---

### The Vinaigrette, By Ratio

| Component        | Ratio   | Notes                              |
| ---------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Olive oil        | 3       | Tries to keep the peace            |
| Red wine vinegar | 1       | Says what everyone's thinking      |
| Dijon mustard    | 1 tsp   | Brings receipts                    |
| Honey            | ½ tsp   | A weak attempt at softening things |
| Garlic, minced   | 1 clove | Lingers longer than invited        |

---

### Assembly Checklist

- [ ] Whisk vinegar, mustard, honey, and garlic together
- [ ] Slowly stream in olive oil, whisking until it's stopped sulking and emulsified
- [ ] Wash and dry the greens thoroughly — soggy greens have no comeback for this dressing
- [ ] Toss greens with just enough vinaigrette to coat, not drown
- [ ] Serve immediately, before the dressing has time to reconsider

> [!WARNING]
> Do not let the salad sit dressed for more than ten minutes. The greens wilt, and the vinaigrette only gets more pointed with time.

---

### Final Thoughts

Sharp, a little too honest, and somehow exactly what the greens needed. Everyone at the table agreed it was good, though nobody wanted to be the first to say so.

Overall rating: **7 out of 10 uncomfortable truths**.
